# `resin`

A small Souls-like game.

Run `./setup.sh` to inflate the project after cloning.

## Story

Dark Souls meets Prey (2017).

- You awaken on an island with a large university building in the distance where time is frozen. 
  University resembles Raya Lucaria. Ancient, but cutting-edge.
- Why are you here?
- The university is uninhabited, except for hollows, monsters, etc.
- Over time, learn lore: university tried to create artificial intelligence.
- Different wings/sections of the university exposit different approaches.
  - E.g. greenhouse with sentient, mobile plants.
  - E.g. workshop with clockwork automata, rule-based systems.
  - E.g. hospital where humans (now long dead) were studied.
  - E.g. boid-like swarms of insects, seeking emergent intelligence.
  - E.g. spirit-channeling embodies animals, chimerae of ghosts. "Black magic", highly taboo.
- After beating all major bosses, building goes into an "emergency protocol". Basement opens.
- Basement reveals horrific truth:
  - University was under pressure to produce results, they became desperate.
  - Decided to capture human souls. Rather than create intelligence, reuse existing intelligence.
  - Secret skunkworks division was hidden deep below university, horrific experiments were 
    conducted to mutilate the consciousness.
  - Player and other hollows were trapped to use for these experiments.
  - Like Frankenstein's monster, they succeeded, but were then eradicated by their creation and 
    forgotten.
  - Now, new souls continue to be trapped and drawn into the university. Most go hollow/insane over
    enough time.
- Final boss: superintelligent amalgam of tortured souls. This entity keeps attracting new souls to
  add to the soul-mass. Exhibits aspects of all the university's schools of thought.
- After defeating final boss, you are finally free. Game over.
